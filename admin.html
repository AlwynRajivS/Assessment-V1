🤖 AI Question Generator

Generate MCQs from a topic or study material using the Groq API. Pick how many questions, which Bloom's Taxonomy levels, and optionally which skill-enhancement question styles you want, then review and choose which ones to add to your test.

Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and is sent directly from your browser to Groq — it never touches the assessment backend. Get a free key at console.groq.com/keys.
Groq periodically retires models — if a model here ever errors as "does not exist or you do not have access", open the Groq Console → Playground to see which model IDs are currently live on your plan, then pick another option from this list.
Supports .txt, .md, .csv, .pdf, .docx (Word) and .pptx (PowerPoint) — text is extracted right here in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere. Legacy .doc/.ppt aren't supported; re-save as .docx/.pptx first, or paste the text directly.
These are optional question-style flavors (Scenario-based, Case Study, Critical Thinking, etc.) layered on top of the Bloom's levels above. They only guide how the AI phrases/generates questions during review — they are not saved as a new field in your backend, so nothing about your existing data model changes.

📋 Quick Start Guide

Setup Steps:
1. Create a test with title and duration
2. Add questions manually, or use "AI Generate" to create MCQs from a topic/material with Groq AI
3. Set test as ACTIVE and configure dates
4. Get link & QR code to share with students
5. Monitor leaderboard in real-time
6. Generate reports after exam window closes
